Bonitah Financial Network (BFN)

Vision: To empower people with financial knowledge and make saving and investing accessible to everyone.

Problem: Many young people lack financial literacy, have limited access to investment opportunities, and struggle to manage their money effectively.

Solution: BFN is a blockchain-powered platform that combines financial education with digital investment tools. It will help users learn about money, save consistently, invest securely, and track their financial progress.

Key Features:

- AI-powered financial assistant for personalized guidance.
- Financial education through articles, videos, seminars, and workshops.
- Secure blockchain-based savings and investment records.
- Community investment groups (SocialFi) where users can save and invest together.
- Goal-based savings for education, business, emergencies, and other personal goals.

Target Users:

- Students
- Young professionals
- Small business owners
- Anyone interested in learning about finance and investing.


Hackathon Focus:

- Consumer AI: Personalized financial education and recommendations.
- DeFi & SocialFi: Transparent digital savings, investments, and community finance using blockchain.

Long-term Goal: To become Africa's leading financial literacy and digital investment platform.
